# STATIK Product Research Synthesis

## Queensland Raceway patterns worth extracting
- Event discovery must be easy and obvious.
- Venue/event taxonomy matters.
- Calendar framing works best when paired with featured event content and broader discovery modules.
- Motorsport facilities make users trust the schedule when event types are clearly segmented.

## Growl Racing patterns worth extracting
- Community language is central, not just vehicles.
- Events and local ecosystem partnerships create repeat reasons to return.
- Industry directory / expo / event hybrid models show monetization beyond merch.

## Beacons patterns worth extracting
- Profile pages work when they are direct, mobile-first, and easy to share.
- Media-kit and analytics increase creator and sponsor value.
- Link stacks become stronger when paired with audience and performance data.

## Strong synthesis for STATIK
- Events become the front door.
- Official updates keep the platform feeling alive.
- Profiles become the identity and conversion layer.
- Submission + moderation create quality control.
- Sponsor placements, promoted events, featured profiles, and brand deals create monetization without making the UI feel ad-heavy.
