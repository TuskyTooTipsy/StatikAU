STATIK deployment notes

Backend runs on Railway from statik-backend.
Frontend runs on Netlify from statik-frontend.
Public canonical domain is https://statik.au.

Backend Railway env:
PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://statik.au
FRONTEND_URL=https://statik.au
ALLOWED_ORIGINS=https://statik.au,https://www.statik.au
PUBLIC_SERVER_URL=https://YOUR-RAILWAY-BACKEND-DOMAIN
ADMIN_EMAIL=...
ADMIN_PASSWORD=...
ADMIN_DISPLAY_NAME=STATIK Admin
JWT_SECRET=...
MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES=41943040
STORAGE_PROVIDER=local
UPLOADS_DIR=/data/uploads

Mount a Railway volume at /data if using local uploads/JSON persistence.

Frontend Netlify env:
VITE_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://statik.au
VITE_API_BASE_URL=https://YOUR-RAILWAY-BACKEND-DOMAIN
VITE_MAPBOX_TOKEN=...

Netlify SPA redirects keep /s/:slug on statik.au. The frontend resolves and tracks shortlinks by calling /api/links/resolve/:slug on the backend.
